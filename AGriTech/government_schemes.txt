PM-KISAN Scheme:
The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme provides income support of ₹6,000 per year to eligible farmer families, paid in three equal installments.

Soil Health Card Scheme:
This scheme helps farmers understand soil nutrient status and recommends appropriate fertilizer usage.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY):
A crop insurance scheme that provides financial support to farmers in case of crop loss due to natural calamities.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC):
Provides farmers with affordable credit for crop production and allied activities.

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY):
Promotes organic farming practices among farmers.

National Agriculture Market (e-NAM):
An online trading platform for agricultural commodities.

Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY):
Supports states in boosting agricultural development.
